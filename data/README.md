Place the dataset CSV in this folder and point `path` in `configs/dataset.json` to it.

Source: S. Oumsaad, M. Tarhouni, S. Zidi, B. Alaya, "VeReMi_Extension: Dataset for Misbehaviors in VANETs",
Mendeley Data, v1, 2023, DOI 10.17632/k62n4z9gdz.1 (https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/k62n4z9gdz/1).
It merges the 39 simulations of the VeReMi Extension (Kamel et al.) into one CSV with 3,194,808 rows.

The CSV is not stored in this repository.
